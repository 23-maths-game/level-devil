❓ HOW TO PLAY

Controls

  • P1: A/D move, W or Space jump
  • P2: ←/→ move, ↑ jump
  • H hint · R restart · M mute
  • Tap ESC = fake pause 😈 (troll)

2-Player rule

  • Shared fate: if one player dies, BOTH restart. Also, both players must stand on the goal together.

⚖️ The Fairness Promise

No random impossible death. Every trap has a TELL — a visual or audio warning before it strikes. Die to a trap once and the game shows you a hint (press H anytime).

😈 Trap tells

  • Crumbling floor — shakes + cracks before it vanishes
  • Coward floor — cracks while you stand; vanishes when you leave
  • Fake platform — dashed outline, slightly transparent
  • Invisible wall — flashes when you bump it
  • Reverse zone — purple + ⇄ icon
  • Camera flip — cyan zone + warning toast
  • Layout shift — orange zone + audible RUMBLE
  • Teleport pad — swirl animation
  • Fake checkpoint — purple flickering flag (real = green, steady)
  • Fake finish — gray flag, no glow (real = glowing portal)
  • Wrong lever — red handle (real = green)
  • Killer door — red pulsing outline
  • Rock spawner — shadow on the ground BEFORE the rock drops
  • Crusher — shadow + warning before it drops
  • Laser beam — bright RED while ON (kills), dim + dashed while OFF (safe)
  • Conveyor belt — glowing arrows show the direction
  • Ice floor — frost-blue with sparkles; you slide, you don't stop
  • Updraft wind — cyan zone with rising arrows; floaty inside
  • One-way door — blue with a green → arrow; forward only
  • Level timer — HUD countdown + ticking in the last 5 seconds
  • Darkness — spotlight around you; traps glow faintly red
  • The Devil (boss) — huge, visible, ROARS before changing direction
  • Bounce pad — yellow spring; it LAUNCHES you (boing!)
  • Pendulum — chain + spiked ball, swings in a visible arc
  • Spike shooter — barrel blinks red, then fires (shots are visible)
  • Rising spikes — cracks + shake right before they pop up
  • Vortex — purple swirl; pulls you toward its center
  • Swap pad — two-color swirl; swaps P1 and P2 bodies (2P)
